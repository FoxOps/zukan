Zukan 図鑑

"Illustrated encyclopedia", like the Pokédex.

Zukan is a free, open-source, self-hosted collection manager for trading card games: Magic: The Gathering, Pokémon TCG, Sorcery: Contested Realm and Riftbound, with a plugin system for more games.

  • Card catalog with every printing, in several languages, with card pictures.
  • Collection tracking by printing, finish, condition and language, with its value over time.
  • Market prices from Cardmarket and TCGplayer.
  • Android app that scans cards with the camera to find them and update the collection.
  • Web interface, multiple users, local accounts or single sign-on (OpenID Connect).

Zukan is not a game: it does not play, simulate or enforce the rules of any card game, has no ranking and sells nothing.

Source code, installation and documentation: github.com/FoxOps/zukan
